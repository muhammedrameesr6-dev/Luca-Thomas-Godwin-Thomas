# Luca Thomas & Godwin Thomas – Baptism & Holy Communion

A bespoke, responsive Christian celebration invitation website designed for the combined ceremony of **Luca Thomas** (Sacrament of Holy Baptism) and **Godwin Thomas** (First Holy Communion).

## Event Details
* **Celebration:** Baptism & First Holy Communion
* **Children:**
  * **Luca Thomas** – Sacrament of Holy Baptism
  * **Godwin Thomas** – First Holy Communion
* **Parents:** Anil Thomas & Preema Lopes
* **Date:** Saturday, 19 December 2026
* **Time:** 5:00 PM
* **Church Sanctuary:** St. Mathew's Church, South Kollencode, Pozhiyoor, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
* **Church Location:** [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hK6WAzEh7v3pxY8k9?g_st=aw)
* **Reception Venue:** Carmel Sports Club / Hub, Pozhiyoor
* **Reception Location:** [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WGrYPNZh3La7R7nu5?g_st=aw)

## Design Features
* **Color System:** Soft Ivory (`#FAF7F0`), Powder Blue (`#DCE9F5`), Pastel Blue (`#AFC7E0`), Blush Pink (`#E8C9C5`), Soft Yellow (`#E9D99D`), Warm Grey (`#77736D`), Deep Charcoal (`#343B45`).
* **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, and Poppins.
* **Hero Arch Showcase:** Featured celebration portrait of the brothers in white suits.
* **Live Countdown Timer:** Symmetrical mobile-friendly countdown to 19 December 2026, 5:00 PM.
* **Invisible Autoplay Audio:** Serene instrumental ambient hymn playing automatically in the background.
* **Calendar Integration:** Google Calendar link & downloadable `.ics` iCal / Outlook invite.
* **Guestbook:** Interactive prayer and blessings submission board persisted locally.
